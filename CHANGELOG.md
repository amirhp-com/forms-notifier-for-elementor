# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Capture of Elementor Pro forms (classic and atomic) and Hello Plus forms, automatic form discovery, an editor action, saved submissions, import of Elementor submissions.
- 19 channels: Telegram, Bale, SMS, WhatsApp, email, webhooks, Slack, Discord, Google Chat, Microsoft Teams, Mattermost, Rocket.Chat, ntfy, Pushover, Gotify, Eitaa, Rubika, Soroush Plus, Gap.
- Telegram and Bale bots with chat discovery, topics, commands, signed action buttons, admin linking, webhook or polling, and a guide for a Cloudflare Worker forwarder.
- Mini App for Telegram and Bale without remote scripts.
- 31 SMS gateways with country routing, backup gateways and pattern text; 10 WhatsApp gateways.
- Template engine with tags, filters and conditions; per-language texts; WPML and Polylang.
- Webhooks with presets for n8n, Make, Zapier, Pabbly Connect and IFTTT, HMAC signatures and stable delivery ids.
- Routing rules, quiet hours, attachments policy with signed links.
- Queue with retries and backoff, message log, backup channel, failure alerts, digest.
- REST API, WP-CLI, hooks, Site Health, privacy tools, import and export, full Persian translation.
